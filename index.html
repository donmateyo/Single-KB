Protocol & Pacing

Format: Descending ladder from 20 to 2, subtracting 2 reps each round. Alternate between Goblet Squats and Kettlebell Swings. Rest only as necessary.

Movement Standard: Goblet squats must reach full depth (hip crease below knee). Kettlebell swings must reach chest height with a powerful hip snap. Do not sacrifice form for speed.

Pacing Strategy: The first four rounds (20, 18, 16, 14) are where most trainees fail by starting too aggressively. Begin at a sustainable pace. The final four rounds (8, 6, 4, 2) should be performed as fast as proper form allows.

Why This Works: The squat-to-swing pairing creates sustained heart-rate elevation. Research shows kettlebell protocols burn ~20 calories per minute and maintain 86-99% of max heart rate.

Progression Protocol

Week 1: Complete the full 20-to-2 ladder. Record total completion time.

Week 2: Target a 10% reduction in total completion time using the same weight.

Week 3: Increase kettlebell weight by one standard increment. Re-establish baseline time.

Week 4+: Alternate between chasing faster times and heavier loads each session.
